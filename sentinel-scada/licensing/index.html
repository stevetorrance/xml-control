04 · The licence, in plain words

The small print, in one place

Perpetual
A licence is a one-time purchase. It does not expire and does not need renewing. Prices on this page are for the 2026/7 season and may change.
One key, one server PC
A licence is hardware-locked to the host system. A second server, such as a standby or test machine, needs its own licence.
Moving to a new server PC
A licence transfer is a fixed fee: £40 for Home, £100 for Standard, £200 for Pro, Clipper and TradeWinds.
Email support
Included with every edition. It covers questions about using SentinelSCADA and bug reports, which are welcome and prioritised. Replies are normally within 48 business hours. It does not cover building or reviewing your project.
On-site support
Help on your own site is £800 per day, plus travel, accommodation and expenses.
TradeWinds on-site setup
TradeWinds includes 3 days on-site: setup support for your IT person, covering installation and Keycloak commissioning, and training for your operators and engineers. All travel, accommodation and expenses are included for one visit to one site in the UK, Europe, or a major city in the US or Canada. Other locations are by arrangement. It does not cover building your project, and the days are to be taken within 12 months of purchase.
Concessions
Concessions are available for students and education providers — contact us for more.
Keycloak (CFR Part 11)
Available from Clipper. It requires Docker and Keycloak configuration, and an IT professional will normally be needed.
Alarm Dispatcher
Alarm notifications are delivered using Telegram Messenger.
“Unlimited”
Unlimited means SentinelSCADA imposes no licence limit. Practical capacity depends on your server hardware, your network, and how many tags change how often.
Evaluation mode
With no licence key installed, SentinelSCADA runs in Evaluation mode. It is fully functional for development and testing, with the limits shown above. The 240-minute runtime counts cumulative Run and Kiosk time per server session; restarting the server resets it.

Clipper and TradeWinds need proper hardware

Clipper and TradeWinds editions require appropriate server-rated hardware and setup for enterprise scaling and security, and a Windows Server operating system is recommended. Contact us if you would like to talk through a deployment before you buy.
