04 · The licence, in plain words

No asterisks worth chasing

Perpetual
The version you buy is yours to run for as long as you like. It does not phone home for permission and it does not stop working.
Updates
Every point release in your major version is free — fixes, new symbols, new report types. When a major version lands, you may move up for 45% of list, or stay exactly where you are.
One key, one PC
Each licence key activates a single computer. The companion key adds a second machine for the same engineer at 25% of list — the bench and the laptop, not two people. It is ordered with the licence, not added afterwards.
Upgrades
Move up the range whenever the work asks for it. You pay the difference between what you paid and the higher tier's list price on the day you upgrade, within your major version. No deadline, and you never buy the same software twice.
Support
The community forum is open to everyone, on every tier, with no time limit. Email support comes with the Publisher Tools — PRO and above — because that is where a job gets complicated. It does not expire either: a perpetual licence with support that lapses is not perpetual.

Support covers the software, not the engineering. How to make a schedule show spare ways is support; whether your motor starter is right is not. And a bug report is welcome from anyone, on any tier, forever — if something is broken, tell us, whatever you paid.
Automation runs
Auto Numbering, the two cross-reference sweeps, Net Runner and Populate Forms are unlimited on every paid licence. An evaluation includes 100 runs in total — far more than trying the tool takes, and they are the only thing in it that is counted.
Your files
Projects are readable files on your own disk. Drawings export to DXF and PDF from every tier, so nothing you draw is trapped in here.

The sheet limit is the limit

Each tier draws up to its sheet count and no further. There is no soft edge and no grace sheet, so choose the tier that matches the jobs you actually take on rather than the one you hope to grow into.

If the work outgrows it, moving up costs only the difference. Buying STD today and PRO whenever you actually need it costs no more than buying PRO outright — there is no window to miss.

And if 400 sheets is not enough, that is not the end of the road either: larger licences are issued on request. Tell us the size of the job and we will size one to it.
